Trailing 12-month reported earnings ÷ monthly average price (inverse of trailing P/E). Dashed line: 30-year average.

Source: Robert J. Shiller, Irrational Exuberance data (monthly)

—

Trailing 12-month dividends ÷ monthly average price.

Source: Robert J. Shiller, Irrational Exuberance data (monthly)

S&P 500 Dividend Growth

—

Trailing 12-month dividends per share, % change from a year earlier, monthly.

Source: Robert J. Shiller, Irrational Exuberance data (monthly)

US Inflation Rate

—

CPI-U (seasonally adjusted), % change from a year earlier, monthly.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics via FRED (CPIAUCSL)

US GDP Growth Rate

—

Nominal GDP (current dollars), % change from a year earlier, quarterly.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis via FRED (GDP)

US Real GDP Growth Rate

—

Real GDP (chained dollars, inflation-adjusted), % change from a year earlier, quarterly.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis via FRED (GDPC1)

10 Year Treasury Rate

—

10-year constant-maturity yield, monthly average; latest point is the most recent daily value.

Source: Federal Reserve Board (H.15) via FRED (GS10) and DGS10
